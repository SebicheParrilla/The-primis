## Theme: brainstorming competition aproach ideas
How will the car move?

- Pneumtic engine and electric motor with a eCVT
  - Compressed air system that can get refilled, last the duration of the round and leak free for eficiency
    - elecronic valves
    - air regulator
    - air pressure sensor
    - lightweight high capacity air tanks

What sensors wil it use to know where to and how to move?
- Raspberry pi board with a camera 
  - needs complicated but possible coding
  - needs a UART connection to a board with a included motor driver like the Cytron Motion 2350

We determined that the components will be purchased and will work on a proof of concept of all of the pneumatic components
