Since we need to supply 12V to the [electronic valve](e-valve.md), we used a voltage booster to make our 7.4V supplied from our batteries to the 12V we need. One can adjust the vltage being outputted by loosening or tightening a potenciometer on the module. It has a 4Amp max which is plenty for the .7Amps its going to withstand
