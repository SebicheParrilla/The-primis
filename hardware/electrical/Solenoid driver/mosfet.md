A (Metal Oxide Semiconductor Field Effect Transistor) is an electronic switching component that has various uses like protecting circuits from surges of current and amplifying signals, but we used it to control a high-current circuit using a low-current control signal.

A MOSFET works by applying a small voltage to the **gate terminal** (Labled **G** in the image), which controls the current flow between the **drain (D)** and **source (S)** terminals. This makes it ideal for switching applications where fast, reliable control of motors, valves, or other actuators is required.
