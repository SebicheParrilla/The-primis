# Air Supply and Management System (ASMS)

The Air supply and management system plays a crutial part in making our vehicle work, it ensures eficient and leak free supply of compressed air to the pneumatic engine. The high pressure side needed to be safe for 100psi, and ur low pressure side needed to be safe for a max of 30 psi,all of ur pneumatic components are rated higher pressure than the most they will ever endure. We knew this system was going to take up a lot of space and weight, so we allways kept in mind picking lightweight and compact components.
