This is our first stable iteration, its 3 7x11 frame on parralel structure made it  compact and symmetrical. It had a run time of 7 seconds on a 40psi tank, using a 2 crankshaft system which conected one another with 3 gears making a 1:1 gear ratio from the crankshafts to the output without a flywheel.

The camshaft levers were on the back of the engine.

This is a version that we liked after many iterations that had various problems, the most notable one was the camshaft lever system, since valves were stiff we had to use it in a way that was low on friction, we also had to take in mind compactness and weight.
