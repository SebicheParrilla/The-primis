## Specs
- Module: 2
- Pressure angle: 20°
- Gear ratio, 1 (Sun) : 4 (planet Carrier)
- R (Ring Gear) = 48
- S (Sun Gear) = 12
- P (Planet Gear) = 18
- OR (Outside Ring Gear) = 60




## Transmission Output/Input

Considering the chanegs to the input and output, now we needed a way to spin the ring gear with the electric transmission motor.

That is why we made another gear on the outside of the ring gear and called it **Outside Ring Gear**.



## Improvements proyected for the E-CVT 2.0

We were proud of our first functional Transmission but it was clear, it was way big for it to fit on a vehicle with all the things we had in mind that had to be in said vehicle, so we aimed to have a smaller and more compact transmission.
