## Why it failed 
Every gear from the past iteration as cut by half, and therefore it was way smaller thatn the previous version.

But we didn't see something coming, since the gears had large teeth compared to the size we had to make a big planet carrier, and we accientaly made it bigger than the teeth on ou ring gear, so it was impossible to assemble.

We desingned the planet carrier with the bearing for the planet gears and the sun gear very close together, when we assembld the planet carrier, it warped and snaped because of metal not stretching nor cmpling with the error of tolerances made by our 3d printer.



## Improvements proyected for the E-CVT 4.0

We learned that we had to keep in mind how we would assemble our designs when printed.

We knew we needed a balance of reducing the mdule and teeth number in order to have proportional gears for the transmission.
