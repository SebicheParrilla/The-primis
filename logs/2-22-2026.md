# Air storage brainstorming
> Retrospective log: This entry's information was digitized from the physical engineering notebook on 2026-07-10.

The pneumatic engine needs a steady flow of 25psi of air pressure for it to work properly, abviously the vehicle must be autonomous and have all of the energy its going to consume onboard. 

We think 90 seconds is a good time for the pneumatic engine to operate, these are our options to make that happen:

- Option #1
Soda bottles inflated to the same pressure that the pneuamtic engine needs.

- Option #2
High pressure, lightweight tanks inflated up to 100psi, but that comes with a problem, 100psi to the pneumatic engine would tear air hoses, so a air regulator is necesary to step down the pressure, this also makes the pressure last longer as it is beign used in a pressure lower than it is being used.
