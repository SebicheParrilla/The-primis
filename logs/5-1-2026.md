# Preparing for the PRNRO
> Retrospective log: This entry's information was digitized from the physical engineering notebook on 2026-06-30.

We are aproaching the day of the national olympiad for th Puerto Rico team, and we are optimizing our robot to go to the competition.

Our pneumatic system will not be available for these competitions, so we adapted a handicap mode for us to compete.

Since the transmission varies the rotating energy from the penumatic engine and the ring gear motor, the motor alone can be used to operate the robot.

using the huskylens as a sensor to detect the orange and blue line to determine the outcome, and the white on the play mat to know when the robot is near a wall.
