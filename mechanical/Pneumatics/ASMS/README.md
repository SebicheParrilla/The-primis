# Air Supply and Management System (ASMS)

The Air supply and management system plays a crutial part in making our vehicle work, it ensures eficient and leak free supply of compressed air to the pneumatic engine. The high pressure side needed to be safe for 100psi, and our low pressure side needed to be safe for a max of 30 psi, all of our pneumatic components are rated higher pressure than the most they will ever endure. We knew this system was going to take up a lot of space and weight, so we allways kept in mind picking lightweight and compact components.

## Air regulator
 After testing with various vertions of our pneumatic engines, we deduced that we needed to supply our engines with 20-25 psi of constant pressure for a minimum of 90 seconds. The regulator allows the robot to store more air in higher pressures to later be used in lower pressure for a longer period of time, visit [air_supply_duration_calculations](../../../../logs/tests/air_supply_duration_calculations.md) for more information.
